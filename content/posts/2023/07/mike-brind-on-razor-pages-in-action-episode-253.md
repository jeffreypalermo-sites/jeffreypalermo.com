Mike Brind spent the first 20 years of his working life in a series of successful sales and marketing roles, towards the end of which he was introduced to HTML and databases. A dormant inner geek took over and Mike became very much more interested in developing websites than selling advertising space on them.

As well as books such as those in the Wrox Beginner series, Mike became reliant on the enormous amount of free help provided by online communities while he learned his new craft. Mike is now one of the all-time leading contributors to the official ASP.NET forums at [http\://forums.asp.net](http://forums.asp.net/) and is also a moderator there.

As a result of his contributions to the ASP.NET community via the forums, and through his technical article site at [http\://www\.mikesdotnetting.com](http://www.mikesdotnetting.com/), Mike received the Microsoft Most Valuable Professional (MVP) Award for ASP.NET from 2008 to 2018. Beginning ASP.NET Web Pages with WebMatrix is Mike's first book.
