Giorgi Dalakishvili is a software developer with more than a decade of experience. He works mainly with C#, [ASP.NET](http://asp.net/) MVC/[ASP.NET](http://asp.net/) Core, REST, WCF, Xamarin, Android, iOS, Entity Framework, Azure, SQL Server, and Oracle.

Giorgi is an open-source author and contributor on GitHub and a member of the .NET Foundation and InfoQ Editor.
