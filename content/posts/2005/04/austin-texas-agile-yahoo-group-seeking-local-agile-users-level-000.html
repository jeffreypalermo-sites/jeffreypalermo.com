If you live around Austin, TX and are interested in Agile development
methodologies, check out http://groups.yahoo.com/group/AgileATX/.

This group is for locals, and we have a biweekly lunch to discuss a wide
range of development topics.  We meet at Mongolian BBQ, which is a great
environment for our information meetings.  Our next meeting will likely contain
a lot of content regarding domain-driven design and designing for testability. 
Come and join us.  Subscribe to the Yahoo group.
