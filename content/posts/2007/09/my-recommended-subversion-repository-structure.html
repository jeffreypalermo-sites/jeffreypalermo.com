I would recommend one line of versioning per repository for most cases.  Here is a sample trunk set up with a VS.Net solution.

root
– trunk
  – bin
    – nant
    – log4net
  – src
    – project 1
    – project 2
    – mysolution.sln
    – CommonAssemblyInfo.cs (for version number, etc)
  – automatedbuild.build
  – clickToBuild.bat
– tags
  – 1.0.0.1
  – 1.0.0.2
  – 1.0.0.3
  – 1.0.3.1
  – 1.0.3.2
  – 1.0.0.4
  – 1.0.0.5
– branches
  – 1.0.0.3-hotfix
