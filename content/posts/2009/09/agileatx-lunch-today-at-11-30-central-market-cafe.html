I have been crazy busy lately, and I have missed quite a few of these lunches.  AgileATX is a great venue to discuss agile software engineering with other developers.

If you aren’t familiar with this group in Austin, TX, go to the Yahoo Group. You can subscribe to the Google Calendar for the group here.

It is important to get to Central Market Cafe promptly at 11:30 because it gets crowded for lunch.  Order your food (or buy from the full deli) and head upstairs.  Look for the folks pulling a bunch of tables together.  Introduce yourself and talk with someone you don’t know.  Build some relationships.

Every 2 weeks at 11:30am on Thursday

Where map

Central Market Cafe, 4001 North Lamar, Austin, TX
