I am hiring for the following position.  This person will report directly to me and work on a team that I am coaching.  If interested, you are applying to be on an extreme programming team.  We are very heavy into automation and frictionless development and deployments. 

—————————————–

Headspring Systems is looking for a talented and exceptional Agile Software Tester. Ideal experience should include extensive test automation and experience with extreme programming (XP).  Browser testing such as Watir or Selenium is desired.

Job Responsibilities

  • Work face-to-face in Austin, TX on a high-speed agile engineering team using one-week iterations.
  • Participate in all activities of development including user story definition, estimation, automated testing, and deployment.
  • Enhance existing automated processes to streamline unit testing, integration testing, database change management, and application deployment.

Job Requirements

  • Bachelor’s in Computer Science or equivalent working experience
  • Minimum of 2 years experience in Microsoft technologies
  • Minimum 1 agile project completed or extensive personal study.
  • Strong ability to analyze business requirements, formulate multiple testing options, and effectively communicate these options to clients
  • Self-motivated – able to work cohesively in a team environment and motivate the team
  • Continuous learner that constantly seeks to improve by such means as reading books and blogs and participating in the development community

Headspring Culture
Our work environment is a relaxed one. Our office is open so it gets a little noisy at times but we like the energy this creates. We like to have fun, but we also work hard, we like to have a balanced work life. Excellent medical benefits are also offered. To learn more about our organization, please visit us at www.headspringsystems.com

To apply:
Please send your resume via email: jobs@headspringsystems.com

In your cover, please include your high/low salary requirements and please explain why this role seems like a good match for you.
